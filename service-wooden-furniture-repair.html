Wooden Furniture Repair

Structural fixes for loose joints, splits, veneer lifts and broken components on solid timber pieces.

Why this service

What you get

  • ✓ Original timber kept wherever it can be saved
  • ✓ Reversible hide glue on antique joinery
  • ✓ Colour-matched fills that disappear into the grain

How we work

  1. Assessment

    We photograph and document every fault before touching the piece.

  2. Disassembly

    Failing joints are opened and old glue cleaned back to bare wood.

  3. Repair

    Splits are pinned, veneer is patched and missing sections cut from matching stock.

  4. Reassembly

    Clamped, squared and cured before any finish work begins.

Materials we use

Hide glue Matched hardwood stock Shellac sticks Hand-cut veneer
