Specialisms

Three crafts kept alive in-house

Skills that are hard to buy in, done at our own benches rather than sent away.

Marquetry & inlay

Missing veneer and inlay cut by hand from matching stock, laid with hide glue so a future restorer can lift it again.

Water & oil gilding

Loose gesso consolidated, bole re-laid and gold leaf matched to the surviving original finish.

Steam-bent repairs

Broken bentwood backs and arms re-formed on our own moulds instead of being replaced with new timber.

How we work

Everything written down

Every conserved piece leaves with a written record: what was found, what was done, which materials were used and how to undo them. It is what makes the work defensible in twenty years, and what insurers and auction houses ask for.

01

Assessment

Photographs, measurements and a documented condition report.

02

Proposal

A fixed price, a method statement and a realistic date.

03

Treatment

Reversible work at our benches, photographed as it goes.

04

Handover

Delivery, care notes and the full record for your files.
