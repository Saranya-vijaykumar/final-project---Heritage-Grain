Turnaround times

Honest timings for each stage, and the three things that most often add a week.

Stage by stage

From collection to delivery

Times below are working days for a single piece in average condition.

  1. Collection & assessment

    1–3 days

    We collect from anywhere in the Bay Area, photograph the piece and send a written assessment.

  2. Quotation & approval

    1–2 days

    A fixed quote with options. Nothing starts until you approve it in writing.

  3. Structural work

    3–14 days

    Joints, frames and missing components. The stage that varies most by piece.

  4. Surface preparation

    2–7 days

    Stripping, filling and levelling. Drying time between steps cannot be rushed.

  5. Finishing & curing

    5–21 days

    Colour, topcoats and polishing, with full cure before handling.

  6. Delivery & install

    1–3 days

    Blanket-wrapped delivery and placement in the room of your choice.

Good to know

What makes a job take longer

Curing time

Finishes need days, not hours. Rushing a topcoat is how a surface fails in year two.

Sourcing

Matching veneer, period brass or a specific fabric can add one to three weeks.

Hidden damage

Woodworm or a previous bad repair is only visible once we open the joints.

Need it sooner? Priority slots cut structural work by roughly 40% for a 25% surcharge, subject to bench space.
