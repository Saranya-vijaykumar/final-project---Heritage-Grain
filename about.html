A workshop built on patience

Founded in 1988 above a cabinet shop on Kilnwright Lane, and still repairing furniture the same way.

Our mission

Keep good furniture out of landfill

Most furniture thrown away is structurally sound. Our job is to make repair the obvious choice — by being quick, transparent about cost, and good enough that the repair disappears.

Vision

A city where heirlooms are repaired rather than replaced, and where craft skills are passed on rather than lost.

History

Started as a one-bench polishing shop. Today: twelve craftspeople, four disciplines and a two-week average turnaround.

Standards

Four rules we don't bend

01

Reversible first

On anything with age, we use materials a future restorer can undo.

02

Honest quotes

Fixed price after assessment. If we get it wrong, that's our problem.

03

Original material

We patch and splice before we replace. Replacement is the last option.

04

Documented work

Photographs at every stage, sent to you when the piece is delivered.
